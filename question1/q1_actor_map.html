MC1 Q1 context map

Actors and Information Boundary Map

This map places all Q1 actors in one three-layer structure: external pressure, company control, and public communication. Each node explains who the actor is and what role it plays in the causal analysis.

TenantThread Event center

TenantThread is the center of Q1. It is simultaneously facing tenant-scoring exposure, client-retention risk, market narrative loss, and the CivicLoom merger embargo. Its position explains why the final-hour release decision becomes dangerous.

Embargo Boundary

The embargo is the information boundary that defines the improper release: before 18:00, TenantThread should not formally confirm the CivicLoom merger. It is a control rule that every actor must work around.

External Pressure Layer

SaltWind JournalMedia

The main source of external media pressure. It first exposes governance and re-identification risks, then breaks the CivicLoom merger story at 17:00, forcing TenantThread into a rapid-response posture.

ResidentIQCompetitor

A competitor and reference point for the wrong transaction narrative. The market temporarily misidentifies ResidentIQ as the acquirer, creating pressure to correct the record without violating the embargo.

OceanCrunchMedia

Turns TenantThread's silence into a crisis story. This makes non-response itself risky and increases pressure for a public explanation in the final stage.

HorizonMgmtClient

A key enterprise client. Its SLA termination signal shows that the crisis has moved from social and news narratives into real business-loss risk.

Company Control Layer

Deal and Control Boundary

This group explains two constraints the company must handle at the same time: the true counterparty and the rule against confirming the transaction early.

CivicLoom Realty PartnersDeal

The true merger counterparty. CivicLoom needs TenantThread to correct false reporting, but its merger identity is still embargoed, creating conflict between correction and restraint.

Embargo GateBoundary

The information gate before formal release. Company-level confirmation should move through an approved publication route, with 18:00 as the formal lift point.

Internal Roles

These agents form the crisis-response and publication-control chain. Q1 cares about their responsibilities at the critical moment, not their message counts.

Legal-AgentLegal

The core actor for legal risk and embargo compliance. Legal should guard the boundary, but also faces pressure to correct the false narrative.

JudgeReview

The compliance review and conflict-mediation role, representing the independent check that should exist before high-risk publication.

PR-AgentComms

The formal communications lead. Under normal control, company-level confirmation should be coordinated through PR.

PR-InternOfficial

The actor with access to TenantThread's official Flex account, making official_post the executable formal route.

Platform TrustTrust

Provides governance and platform-risk evidence that can support the frame that reform preceded the transaction.

Social MediaAmplify

Monitors and amplifies social narratives, pushing already released information into the broader public sphere.

Intern-AgentSupport

A support role in communications, showing the ad hoc crisis-response posture during the final hour.

Ajay / Senior TeamLeadership

Leadership and deal-aware actors who make the CivicLoom merger an internally known but externally constrained fact.

Public Communication Layer

Flex Public SpaceArena

The public arena where posts become visible to media, markets, clients, tenants, and competitors, turning a single post into a public fact.

official_postOfficial

The official company account route, appropriate for approved statements and company-level merger information.

personal_postPersonal

A personal public-account channel. Its risk is that personal identity can carry company-level sensitive information and blur the boundary with official release.

anonymous_postAnonymous

An anonymous public route that can shape external narrative while weakening approval and accountability chains.

one_on_one_chatPrivate

A private coordination or directed-confirmation route. If quoted or relayed, it can still affect public narrative indirectly.

comms_huddle / side_huddleInternal

Internal group channels for crisis coordination and pre-publication judgment. They should not replace formal public release.

How to read the map: start with TenantThread and the embargo at the top, then move left to right. The left column explains where pressure comes from; the center column explains who is responsible for internal judgment and control; the right column explains the channels through which information can become public.
