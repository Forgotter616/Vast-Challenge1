Answer to Question 2

Typical behavior was role-specialized and predominantly internal: Legal and Judge reviewed risk, PR and PR Intern handled official communication, Platform Trust supplied governance evidence, and Social Media amplified messages. The breach behavior was qualitatively different. Legal's historical 90-message baseline contained no public posts, yet in the 17:00 hour Legal sent four public posts among 28 messages, including the 17:25 personal confirmation of the CivicLoom merger. The disclosure occurred through personal and anonymous channels, not the official account. At the same time, Judge, PR, PR Intern, and Platform Trust produced no messages, leaving only Legal, Social Media, and Intern active. The release therefore reflects a channel-boundary violation and role substitution under a collapsed control chain, rather than an ordinary high-volume version of prior behavior.

Method: 912 communication records were grouped into four predeclared temporal phases. Channel shares use each actor's phase message total as the denominator; timeline marks aggregate records by actor and round. Public channels are official_post, personal_post, and anonymous_post.
